Pertanyaan umum

Bagaimana cara menghapus akun?
Buka app › ikon profil di Beranda › Hapus akun. Profil, match, dan pesanmu terhapus dari server kami.

Bagaimana cara membatalkan GymMate Plus?
Pengaturan iPhone › nama kamu (Akun Apple) › Langganan › GymMate › Batalkan Langganan. Menghapus app atau akun tidak membatalkan langganan Apple.

Bagaimana melaporkan pengguna?
Ketuk ••• di profil atau chat › Laporkan. Laporan anonim dan ditinjau dalam 24 jam. Dalam keadaan darurat, hubungi petugas gym atau 112.

Need help?

Email us at gymmateapp@outlook.com. We reply within 3 business days. Please include the app version (bottom of the Profile screen) when reporting a problem.

FAQ

How do I delete my account?
Open the app › profile icon on Home › Delete account. Your profile, matches and messages are removed from our servers.

How do I cancel GymMate Plus?
iPhone Settings › your name (Apple Account) › Subscriptions › GymMate › Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I report a user?
Tap ••• on a profile or chat › Report. Reports are anonymous and reviewed within 24 hours. In an emergency, contact gym staff or call 112.
